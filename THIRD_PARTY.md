## Compatible USB bar-display protocol

OwnDash 0.6 contains a newly structured USB backend for the controller currently identified as USB `33c3:0e02`. Protocol interoperability details such as USB identifiers, packet constants and the firmware authentication public key were informed by the publicly available reverse-engineering work in **hevnsnt/artinchip-linux**.
